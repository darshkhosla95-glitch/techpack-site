Tech Pack Maker

A private tool for writing garment tech packs and building them as branded PowerPoint decks.

Open Tech Pack Maker

What it does

  • Guides you through a garment's tech pack page by page: style and design overviews, construction, fabric, colourways, measurements and size specification, branding and labels, and the bill of materials.
  • Keeps the artwork you upload (SVG, PNG, JPEG or PDF) with each pack, and checks every value is filled in or marked TBC.
  • Lets you review and approve each slide, and keeps checkpoints you can go back to.
  • Builds the tech pack as a PowerPoint deck in the house style, checks its header, footer and typography, and saves a copy to Google Drive.

Who can use it

Tech Pack Maker is a private tool, not a public service. Only Google accounts its owner has invited can sign in; anyone else is refused at Google's sign-in.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

You sign in with your Google account, which tells the app your email address and nothing else. Finished decks and a weekly backup are saved to the owner's Google Drive, with access to files the app creates there only (Google's drive.file permission). It can't see or change anything else in that Drive. Read the privacy policy for what's stored and where.
